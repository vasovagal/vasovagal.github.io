ZERO-TOUCH CAPTURE

INSTALL IT.
JOIN A CALL.
THAT'S THE SETUP.

Corti watches macOS's microphone-in-use signal. When Zoom, Slack, Google Meet, Discord, or another app grabs the mic, capture begins automatically. The owning process becomes the source tag on the note.

  • [AUTO] identifies whichever app owns the microphone
  • [DUAL] captures your mic and far-end audio separately
  • [LOCAL] runs Parakeet on CPU or Metal
  • [LIVE] shows timestamped Me / Them transcript rows
  • [DURABLE] streams bounded writes into the Vagus inbox

NO CALENDAR HOOK. NO “JOIN MEETING” BOT. NO ACCOUNT.

LIVE WRITE AUTHORITY

THE NOTE EXISTS
BEFORE THE CALL ENDS.

With the local backend, Corti appends a bounded window every 1–10 minutes. Each complete chunk is OS-synced before memory is reused. A crash can lose the active interval—not the whole conversation.
