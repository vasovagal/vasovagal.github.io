AUTOMATIC CAPTURE

SET UP ONCE.
JOIN A CALL.
CAPTURE AUTOMATICALLY.

Grant Microphone and System Audio Recording permissions, then configure transcription in Settings. Corti watches microphone activity to start and stop capture. It tags the note with the detected app; attribution can fall back to the frontmost app or an unknown label.

  • [AUTO] detects sustained microphone activity
  • [DUAL] captures your mic and far-end audio separately
  • [LOCAL] runs Parakeet on CPU or Metal
  • [LIVE] shows timestamped Me / Them transcript rows
  • [DURABLE] streams bounded writes into the Vagus inbox

NO CALENDAR HOOK. NO MEETING BOT. LOCAL MODE NEEDS NO SERVICE ACCOUNT.

LIVE WRITE AUTHORITY

SAVE THE NOTE
DURING THE CALL.

With live filing enabled and local models ready, Corti buffers 1–10 minutes per transcript window; the default is one minute. Each completed chunk is OS-synced before its memory is reused. A crash can lose the active window; committed chunks remain.

State: transcribing → State: transcribed

FOLLOW THE
LIVE TRANSCRIPT.

The reader shows call-relative timestamps and Me / Them labels as speech regions close. While idle, use its microphone test to check local transcription without saving audio or creating a note.

LOCAL MODE

CHOOSE LOCAL
OR HOSTED PROCESSING.

Local Parakeet transcription runs on CPU or Metal after you download the selected models. Settings also controls far-end speaker labels, echo cancellation, retention, and live filing cadence.

AWS Transcribe uploads audio when selected. Separately enabled hosted cleanup and assistant features send transcript text and prompts to the configured provider. Notes may also sync through your chosen vault service.
